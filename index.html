Supreme Court quashes FIR under 498A, rules complaint filed after 12-year delay "inherently suspect"
Acquittal / Quashing⚖ Supreme Court of IndiaJustice B.R. Gavai & Justice Augustine George Masih
  • Bench held that a 12-year delay in filing dowry harassment FIR without explanation casts serious doubt on genuineness of allegations.
  • Court reiterated its 2017 Rajesh Sharma guidelines — magistrates must refer cases to Family Welfare Committees before arrests.
  • Husband and in-laws had remained in custody for 14 months; SC directed immediate release and expunging of records.
  • Judgment strengthens judicial scrutiny against weaponisation of Section 498A in matrimonial disputes.

Conviction⚖ Delhi High CourtJustice Swarana Kanta Sharma
  • Woman died within seven years of marriage due to burns; court found dowry demand of ₹10 lakh and gold jewellery proven on record.

Recusal controversy: SC judge declines to recuse from case involving company where family member holds directorship Ongoing
Conflict of InterestSupreme Court of India
  • Bar Association of India filed representation citing conflict of interest; Judge acknowledged the connection but said no bias would result.
  • India has no statutory disclosure requirement for judges' financial interests or family connections to litigants.
  • UK, USA, and Australia mandate annual asset declarations and mandatory recusal on family conflict; India relies on judicial discretion alone.
  • RTI applications for judges' asset declarations consistently rejected by SC citing "judicial independence" — no external audit mechanism exists.

Why don't retail prices fall when crude falls?

► Excise + VAT = ~55% of petrol price creates a price floor — any fall in crude is absorbed by OMC margins, not passed to consumers.

► Iran War 2026 spike: Brent hit $90+ in May; 4 hikes in 2 weeks added ~₹8/L on petrol, ₹7/L on diesel.

► Covid excise grab (2020): Centre hiked excise by ₹13/L when crude was $19/bbl. Never reversed.

Bottom Line: India's fuel price is a political instrument, not a market price.
